# Remaining work

1. **Polynomial-time Hungarian algorithm.** Part XVI provides the matching infrastructure (cost matrix, weight, injectivity, max-weight-matching-exists); Part XIX delivers a constructive — though exponential-time — optimal matcher `brute_match` via permutation enumeration, with provable optimality among reordering-derived matchings (`brute_match_optimal_among_perms` via `permutation_in_perms`). The polynomial-time `O(n³)` Hungarian constructor with potentials, alternating trees, and augmenting paths remains the missing implementation. The principal blocker is formalizing LP duality for the assignment problem (or Berge's theorem on the equality subgraph), neither of which is in Stdlib; building the necessary graph-theoretic infrastructure is the natural path.
